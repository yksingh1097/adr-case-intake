Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a dental surgeon and I would like to report a suspected adverse reaction to amoxicillin in a patient of mine.

The patient is a 54-year-old woman, initials A.S., in good general health, who was prescribed amoxicillin 500 mg capsules, one capsule by mouth every eight hours for seven days, following the extraction of a lower molar on 11 May 2026. She took the first capsule the same evening.

On 14 May 2026 she telephoned the clinic to say that she had an itchy red rash on her chest, back and arms. I saw her the same day: there was a confluent erythematous maculopapular rash on the trunk and upper limbs. There was no swelling of the face or lips, no difficulty breathing and no fever. I advised her to stop the amoxicillin, prescribed oral cetirizine 10 mg once daily for five days and switched the antibiotic to clindamycin.

At review on 20 May 2026 the rash had disappeared completely. She was not admitted to hospital and did not require any other treatment. She takes no regular medication.

I consider the rash to be probably related to amoxicillin.
