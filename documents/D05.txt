CLINICAL NARRATIVE
This 45-year-old gentleman was commenced on clozapine in February 2026 after failing adequate trials of two other antipsychotics. Mandatory haematological monitoring was carried out weekly. White cell count and absolute neutrophil count were within the normal range at each visit until week 8.

At the week 9 visit on 6 April 2026 the absolute neutrophil count was 0.3 x 10^9/L (white cell count 1.8 x 10^9/L). The patient reported a sore throat and fever of 38.6 degrees Celsius for two days. He was admitted to the acute medical unit the same day. Clozapine was ceased immediately. Blood cultures were taken and empirical intravenous piperacillin-tazobactam was started. Filgrastim was commenced on the advice of haematology.

PROGRESS
Neutrophil count on 10 April 2026: 0.9 x 10^9/L and rising. Fever has settled. The patient remains an inpatient and is improving. Clozapine will not be restarted.

ASSESSMENT
Agranulocytosis with neutropenic fever, causally related to clozapine (certain). Reported to the national clozapine monitoring registry.
